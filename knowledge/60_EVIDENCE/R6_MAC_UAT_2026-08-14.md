## Defects discovered and resolved during this UAT campaign
The operator UAT usefully exposed three defects/semantic mismatches before any broker write was enabled:

1. A blocked crypto attempt could leave previous-pair metrics visible. The UI now invalidates and clears stale results before each attempt and on pair changes.
2. The rehearsal initially used crypto orderbook event-time as a quote freshness proxy. It now uses the dedicated Alpaca latest-quote and latest-trade contracts.
3. The first quote/trade implementation treated every latest broker event as if it had to occur within the local 60-second snapshot freshness window. Actual Mac observations showed BTC could have a fresh quote with an older latest trade while ETH could show the inverse. The corrected contract distinguishes fresh REST receipt-time from broker event-time, requires a recent activity witness, imposes a hard maximum age on the quieter component, and retains price-coherence/future-time fail-closed checks.

The final build tested here is the corrected build after all three findings.
